# AI Engineering Education

This section is for studying AI Engineering topics from PDFs and other learning materials.

## Learning workflow

1. Share a PDF or other source material for a topic.
2. Work through the topic with a clear, thorough explanation and examples.
3. Add a concise, well-organized summary of the key ideas to this section on GitHub.

This folder can also grow to include useful learning resources and supporting material as the AI Engineering area develops.
