# AI Engineering

This area begins with the [DataCamp Associate AI Engineer for Developers track](https://app.datacamp.com/learn/career-tracks/associate-ai-engineer-for-developers). It will grow over time with notes, exercises, experiments, and projects from the track and from other AI Engineering resources and hands-on work.

## Structure

- `notes/` — concepts, summaries, and references
- `exercises/` — practice problems and solutions
- `experiments/` — focused explorations and prototypes
- `projects/` — larger applied projects
