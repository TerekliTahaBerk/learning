## Özne eylemi yapıyor mu?

`The team examined the sample.` → `The sample was examined (by the team).` Nesne özne olur; tense korunur. Yapan bilinmiyorsa veya sonuç önemliyse by-agent gereksizdir. Her fiil passive olmaz: `happen, arrive, occur` geçişsiz anlamlarında doğrudan nesne almaz; `was happened` yanlış.

| Tense/yapı | Passive | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | am/is/are + V3 | Each sample is labelled. |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are being + V3 | The bridge is being inspected. |
| Present Perfect | have/has been + V3 | The results have been published. |
| Past Simple | was/were + V3 | The site was abandoned. |
| Past Continuous | was/were being + V3 | The files were being copied when the power failed. |
| Past Perfect | had been + V3 | The notice had been sent before the meeting. |
| Future | will be + V3 | The report will be released. |
| Future Perfect | will have been + V3 | By Friday, all samples will have been tested. |
| Modal | modal + be + V3 | The figures must be checked. |
| Modal perfect | modal + have been + V3 | The data might have been altered. |

Perfect continuous passive (`has been being tested`) mümkündür ancak ağır ve seyrektir; çoğu durumda active veya farklı ifade seçilir. `been` ile `being`i sırf şekline bakarak ayırma: `has been tested` bitmiş/şimdiye bağlı passive; `is being tested` sürmekte olan passive.

## Passive infinitive ve gerund

`to be examined` eşzamanlı/sonraki passive: The object needs to be examined.
`to have been examined` daha önceki passive: The object appears to have been examined.
`being examined`: She dislikes being examined in public.
`having been examined`: Having been examined carefully, the manuscript was returned.

## Reporting structures

`People believe that the method is reliable.`
→ `It is believed that the method is reliable.`
→ `The method is believed to be reliable.`

| Raporlama | Örnek | Zaman ilişkisi |
|---|---|---|
| is believed/thought to | The site is thought to contain older remains. | Güncel inanç, eşzamanlı içerik |
| is known to | The compound is known to react with water. | Bilinen özellik |
| is expected to | The policy is expected to reduce waste. | Beklenen sonraki etki |
| is believed to have V3 | The community is believed to have migrated earlier. | İnançtan önceki eylem |
| is thought to have been V3 | The building is thought to have been damaged in a fire. | Daha eski passive olay |

## Sık Tuzak

`The sample is believed that…` iki yapıyı karıştırır. `It is believed that…` veya `The sample is believed to…` seç. Passive dönüşümde kimin kimi etkilediğini, olumsuzluğu ve modalı koru. [Relative reduction](08-participles-reduction.md) içindeki V3 her zaman çekimli passive yüklem değildir.
