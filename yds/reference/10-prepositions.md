## Gramatik ilişkiler

| Alan | Ayrım | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Zaman | at nokta, on gün/tarih, in dönem | at noon; on Monday; in June |
| Süre | for uzunluk; since başlangıç | for two years; since 2020 |
| Sınır | by son tarih; until süre boyunca | finish by Friday; stay until Friday |
| Geçen süre | during + isim; while + clause | during the survey; while we surveyed the site |
| Yer | at nokta/etkinlik; in alan; on yüzey | at the conference; in the laboratory; on the table |
| Yön | to hedef; into içine; onto üstüne | move into the chamber |
| Araç/yapan | with araç; by passive yapan | measured with a sensor; measured by researchers |
| Ayrım | between ayrık ilişkiler; among grup içinde | a link between three named variables; cooperation among residents |

Between yalnızca ikiyle sınırlı değildir; ayrı ayrı tanımlı ilişkilerde üçten fazla ile de kullanılabilir. Türkçe hâl ekinden tek bir İngilizce edat türetme. `discuss the issue` doğrudan nesne alır, `discuss about` standart bu anlamda yanlış; `explain the result to us`.

## Lexical combinations

| Kalıp | Anlam | Özgün cümle |
|---|---|---|
| depend on | bağlı olmak | Success depends on reliable measurements. |
| result in | sonucuna yol açmak | Heat may result in structural damage. |
| result from | kaynaklanmak | The damage resulted from excessive heat. |
| account for | açıklamak / payını oluşturmak | Transport accounts for a large share of costs. |
| contribute to | katkıda bulunmak | Training contributes to reducing errors. |
| lead to | yol açmak | Delays can lead to higher costs. |
| consist of | oluşmak | The sample consists of three layers. |
| comply with | kurala uymak | All facilities must comply with safety rules. |
| associated with | ilişkili | This pattern is associated with higher rainfall. |
| responsible for | sorumlu / neden olan | Several processes are responsible for the change. |
| prevent…from | engellemek | The cover prevents dust from entering. |
| distinguish…from | ayırt etmek | The test distinguishes noise from useful signals. |
| susceptible to | duyarlı / açık | The material is susceptible to corrosion. |
| benefit from | yararlanmak | Smaller schools may benefit from shared equipment. |

## Sık Tuzak

`to` edat olan kalıplarda V-ing: contribute to improving. `responsible of` değil for; `consist from` değil of. `associated with` ilişki bildirir; nedensellik kanıtlamaz. `an increase in prices` artan değişken; `an increase of 10%` artış miktarı; `increase by 10% / to 110` değişim/son nokta.
