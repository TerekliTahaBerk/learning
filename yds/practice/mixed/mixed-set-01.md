## Soru 13

### Reading — Volunteer archive

An archive allowed volunteers to label digital photographs. Each image was reviewed by two volunteers, and disagreements were sent to an experienced editor. This arrangement reduced obvious labelling errors, but it did not remove all uncertainty. Some photographs lacked dates or location details, so even agreement between volunteers could not guarantee a correct label. The archive therefore kept uncertain labels marked as provisional. It also invited users to submit further evidence rather than silently replacing one guess with another.

Why could agreement between volunteers be misleading?

- **A)** All photographs had complete labels from the start.
- **B)** The editor never reviewed disagreements.
- **C)** Two volunteers always have opposite views.
- **D)** Users were forbidden to submit any evidence.
- **E)** Relevant dates or location details were sometimes missing.
