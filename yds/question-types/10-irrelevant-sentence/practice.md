## Soru 1

Which sentence disrupts the paragraph?

(I) The city wanted to reduce water loss from damaged pipes. (II) It installed sensors that detected unusual flows. (III) The city’s oldest water supply came from a spring outside the old walls. (IV) These sensors alerted staff to possible leaks. (V) Repairs could therefore begin sooner.

- **A)** IV
- **B)** V
- **C)** I
- **D)** II
- **E)** III

## Soru 2

(I) Archaeologists found that exposed metal objects deteriorated rapidly. (II) They therefore changed the storage conditions. (III) The new rooms kept humidity at a stable level. (IV) Metalworking was an important craft in many ancient societies. (V) This stability slowed further deterioration of the stored objects.

- **B)** I
- **C)** II
- **D)** III
- **E)** IV

## Soru 3

Which sentence disrupts the paragraph?

(I) Students often confuse familiarity with understanding. (II) A text may seem easy because its wording has been seen repeatedly. (III) Asking learners to explain the idea without the text can expose this confusion. (IV) Such tasks make gaps in understanding more visible. (V) School buildings vary greatly in architectural style.

- **A)** I
- **B)** II
- **C)** III
- **D)** IV
- **E)** V

## Soru 4

Which sentence disrupts the paragraph?

(I) A clinic found that missing records weakened a digital tool's recommendations. (II) It required staff to review recommendations before contacting patients. (III) This review reduced the risk of decisions based on incomplete information. (IV) The clinic was named after a physician who lived a century earlier. (V) The extra safeguard was therefore retained despite its small cost in time.

- **A)** II
- **B)** III
- **C)** IV
- **D)** V
- **E)** I
