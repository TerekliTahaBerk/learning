## Soru 1

### Metin A — Urban monitoring

A city introduced sensors to monitor water use. The devices (1) ___ data every hour, allowing staff to compare daily patterns. Their main purpose was to (2) ___ leaks before they became serious. (3) ___ the initial cost was high, the city continued the programme because early repairs reduced water loss. This reduction contributed (4) ___ lower operating costs. The officials stressed, however, that the results depended on regular maintenance; without (5) ___, the sensors could become unreliable.

(1) için seç.

- **A)** were transmitted
- **B)** transmitted
- **C)** transmission
- **D)** transmitting
- **E)** to transmit

The programme encouraged students (7) ___ their answers before requesting help. The first school reported fewer repeated errors. (8) ___, the second school showed little change. The researchers attributed this difference to irregular use rather than to the system itself. They therefore warned against drawing a firm (9) ___ from the initial results.
