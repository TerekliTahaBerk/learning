## Soru 1

A: We cannot explain why the figures differ.

B: ___

A: Yes, checking whether both teams used the same definition would be a good first step.

- **A)** Should we publish the conclusion without checking anything?
- **B)** Did both teams refuse to collect any figures?
- **C)** Could the teams have defined the category differently?
- **D)** Why did you decide the figures were identical?
- **E)** Are you certain that definitions never matter?

## Soru 2

A: Is the new system definitely more accurate?

B: ___

A: Then we should wait for a larger evaluation before claiming that it is better.

- **A)** Accuracy is irrelevant because all systems work equally well.
- **B)** The initial results are promising, but the sample is too small to be certain.
- **C)** Yes, its superiority has been proved in every setting.
- **D)** No, the old system has never been tested either.
- **E)** The evaluation was completed in every department years ago.

## Soru 3

A: The report says the policy was ineffective.

B: ___

A: You are right; it says the effect was limited to a few regions, not that there was no effect.

- **A)** Does it say there was no effect, or only that the effect was uneven?
- **B)** Why did the policy affect every region equally?
- **C)** Could we assume that every limited effect is harmful?
- **D)** Are the authors claiming there were no regions to study?
- **E)** When did all the regions stop following the policy?

## Soru 4

A: I need access to the archive, but I do not know the procedure.

B: ___

A: Thanks. I will contact the office before travelling.

- **A)** You must have visited the archive last week.
- **B)** The documents were written before the archive opened.
- **C)** Travelling is always cheaper than sending a message.
- **D)** I cannot explain why the archive was founded.
- **E)** You should ask the archive office which documents are required.
