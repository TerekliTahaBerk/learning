Sorular 1–5 Metin A; 6–10 Metin B. Metni tüm boşluklarıyla önce oku. Her boşluk ayrı puanlanır.

Cevaplarını, güven düzeyini ve kanıtını ayrı kâğıda yaz. Yanıt anahtarını bitirince aç.

Soru 1

Metin A — Urban monitoring

A city introduced sensors to monitor water use. The devices (1) ___ data every hour, allowing staff to compare daily patterns. Their main purpose was to (2) ___ leaks before they became serious. (3) ___ the initial cost was high, the city continued the programme because early repairs reduced water loss. This reduction contributed (4) ___ lower operating costs. The officials stressed, however, that the results depended on regular maintenance; without (5) ___, the sensors could become unreliable.

(1) için seç.

  • A) were transmitted
  • B) transmitted
  • C) transmission
  • D) transmitting
  • E) to transmit

Soru 6

Metin B — Classroom feedback

An educational team tested a feedback system in two schools. Before the trial began, teachers (6) ___ training in how to use it. The programme encouraged students (7) ___ their answers before requesting help. The first school reported fewer repeated errors. (8) ___, the second school showed little change. The researchers attributed this difference to irregular use rather than to the system itself. They therefore warned against drawing a firm (9) ___ from the initial results. A larger trial would be required (10) ___ determine whether the effect was consistent.

  • A) receive
  • B) had received
  • C) will receive
  • D) have receiving
  • E) are received
