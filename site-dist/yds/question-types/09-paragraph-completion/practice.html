Paragraf Tamamlama Uygulaması

Ders · Tür dizini

Cevaplarını, güven düzeyini ve kanıtını ayrı kâğıda yaz. Yanıt anahtarını bitirince aç.

Soru 1

Choose the missing sentence:

The museum digitised its most fragile records. ___. This allowed researchers to consult them without handling the originals.

  • A) The fragile records had never contained any useful information.
  • B) The digital copies were made available through a secure online catalogue.
  • C) The records were then removed from all catalogues permanently.
  • D) The museum sold its only computers to reduce costs.
  • E) Researchers were required to handle every original before viewing a copy.

Soru 2

Choose the missing sentence:

The town had frequent power cuts during storms. ___. These reserves kept the emergency centre operating until the main supply returned.

  • A) It therefore installed batteries that stored electricity for emergencies.
  • B) It therefore removed all backup equipment from the centre.
  • C) Storms are often mentioned in local poems.
  • D) The main supply never failed during any storm.
  • E) The centre stopped storing energy because power cuts were impossible.

Soru 3

Choose the missing sentence:

Early studies described the device as highly efficient. ___. The later trials therefore focused on whether it could perform reliably in wet conditions.

  • A) Consequently, efficiency was proved under every possible condition.
  • B) The device had already failed in every dry laboratory.
  • C) All later trials had been cancelled before the early studies began.
  • D) Wet conditions are unrelated to any use of the device.
  • E) However, those studies had tested it only in dry laboratories.

Soru 4

Choose the missing sentence:

A feedback programme reduced repeated errors in one class. The school then tried the programme in three more classes. ___. The staff concluded that implementation mattered as much as the programme itself.

  • A) No class used the programme, yet all effects were attributed to regular use.
  • B) The staff stopped collecting information before any class tried it.
  • C) The number of desks was identical in all classes and was the only factor considered.
  • D) Classes using it regularly improved, whereas classes using it rarely showed little change.
  • E) Every class improved equally regardless of how the programme was used.
