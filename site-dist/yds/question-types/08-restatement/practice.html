Yakın Anlam Uygulaması

Ders · Tür dizini

Cevaplarını, güven düzeyini ve kanıtını ayrı kâğıda yaz. Yanıt anahtarını bitirince aç.

Soru 1

Choose the closest meaning:

The team will publish the results only if the data pass an independent review.

  • A) Passing an independent review is a necessary condition for publication.
  • B) Passing the review guarantees publication regardless of other conditions.
  • C) The results will be published even if the data fail the review.
  • D) Publication must take place before any review can begin.
  • E) The data have already failed a review, so publication is certain.

Soru 2

Choose the closest meaning:

Despite the increase in costs, the project continued.

  • A) The project continued because costs had risen.
  • B) The project stopped after costs rose.
  • C) Costs rose because the project had stopped.
  • D) The project could continue only if costs fell.
  • E) The project continued although costs had risen.

Soru 3

Choose the closest meaning:

The new evidence may challenge the earlier explanation.

  • A) The earlier explanation must be accepted because of the new evidence.
  • B) The new evidence cannot affect any earlier explanation.
  • C) The earlier explanation caused the evidence to be destroyed.
  • D) It is possible that the earlier explanation will be questioned in light of the new evidence.
  • E) The evidence has conclusively disproved every earlier explanation.

Soru 4

Choose the closest meaning:

The researchers did not release the figures until the review was completed.

  • A) The figures were never released, even after the review.
  • B) The review was abandoned because the figures were released.
  • C) The figures were released only after completion of the review.
  • D) The figures were released before the review had begun.
  • E) The researchers completed the review only after releasing the figures.
