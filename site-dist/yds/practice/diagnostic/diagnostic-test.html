Tanılayıcı Test — 24 Soru

Uygulama merkezi · Analiz

İlk denemede sözlük kullanma. Başlangıç süre önerisi 45 dakika; bitiremezsen süreyi ve kalan soruları kaydet, sonra süresiz tamamla. Her soru 1 puan; bu sonuç YDS puan tahmini değildir.

Cevaplarını, güven düzeyini ve kanıtını ayrı kâğıda yaz. Yanıt anahtarını bitirince aç.

Soru 1

The findings ___ that the policy had only a limited effect.

  • A) allocate
  • B) inherit
  • C) comply
  • D) deplete
  • E) indicate

Soru 2

The committee decided to ___ the complaint before issuing a final response.

  • A) give rise to
  • B) phase out
  • C) turn out
  • D) look into
  • E) bring about

Soru 3

The laboratory ___ closed in 2019 and has not reopened.

  • A) will be
  • B) is being
  • C) was
  • D) has been
  • E) had being

Soru 4

The researcher ___ article was cited has agreed to speak at the conference.

  • A) that
  • B) whose
  • C) whom
  • D) which
  • E) where

Soru 5

We postponed ___ the results until the additional checks were complete.

  • A) publishing
  • B) to publish
  • C) publish
  • D) published
  • E) to publishing

Soru 6

The failure resulted ___ a lack of maintenance.

  • A) in
  • B) to
  • C) of
  • D) at
  • E) from

Soru 7

Researchers compared two methods of preserving paper. Both reduced damage, but the cheaper method required more frequent checks. (7) ___, its total cost was not necessarily lower. The team stressed that maintenance should be taken (8) ___ account when comparing the methods.

(7) için seç.

  • A) For example
  • B) In contrast
  • C) Meanwhile
  • D) Consequently
  • E) Similarly

Soru 8

(8) için seç.

  • A) from
  • B) with
  • C) into
  • D) for
  • E) on

Soru 9

Because the sample was not representative, ___ .

  • A) no comparison would ever be needed in any later study
  • B) the results could not be generalised confidently
  • C) every region was certainly represented equally
  • D) the study proved the method worked in all schools
  • E) the sample therefore represented the entire population perfectly

Soru 10

Although the new device is slower, ___ .

  • A) some teams prefer it because its measurements are more consistent
  • B) all teams reject it solely because it is slow
  • C) its speed is lower than that of the old device
  • D) it takes longer to produce each result
  • E) no possible benefit could justify its use

Soru 11

En yakın Türkçe çeviriyi seç:

The intervention might have reduced the risk, but the evidence is insufficient.

  • A) Müdahale riski azaltmak zorundaydı ve kanıt yeterlidir.
  • B) Kanıt yetersiz olduğu için müdahale riski kesinlikle artırdı.
  • C) Müdahale gelecekte riski tamamen ortadan kaldıracaktır.
  • D) Müdahale riski azaltmadığı için kanıt toplanmadı.
  • E) Müdahale riski azaltmış olabilir, ancak kanıt yetersizdir.

Soru 12

En yakın İngilizce çeviriyi seç:

Yerel olarak toplanan örnekler henüz incelenmedi.

  • A) The local samples were examined before collection.
  • B) The samples will never be examined locally.
  • C) Only local researchers examined the samples.
  • D) The samples collected locally have not been examined yet.
  • E) The researchers have not collected any local samples yet.

Soru 13

Reading — Library workshops

A town library offered free evening workshops on evaluating online information. Attendance rose during the first month, but most participants already used the library regularly. Staff could not tell whether the workshops were reaching residents who rarely visited. They therefore asked several community centres to advertise the sessions. Attendance from new visitors increased afterwards, although the staff noted that seasonal changes might also have affected attendance. The library planned to compare attendance over a longer period before deciding which form of advertising worked best.

Which issue motivated advertising through community centres?

  • A) The decision to close every community centre.
  • B) The fact that seasonal changes never affect attendance.
  • C) Uncertainty about reaching residents who rarely visited the library.
  • D) A complete absence of workshop participants.
  • E) Proof that all regular visitors disliked the sessions.

Soru 14

Reading: What does "They" in "They therefore asked" refer to?

  • A) all residents who never visited
  • B) the library staff
  • C) the workshops
  • D) seasonal changes
  • E) the centres themselves

Soru 15

Reading: Which inference is supported?

  • A) The increase in new visitors cannot yet be assigned solely to advertising.
  • B) Advertising has been proved completely ineffective.
  • C) Every new visitor attended because of the same advertisement.
  • D) Seasonal changes were proved to be the only cause.
  • E) The library had already completed a long-term comparison.

Soru 16

Reading: What is the main purpose of the final sentence?

  • A) To announce that all advertising methods are identical.
  • B) To prove that the workshops failed to teach any skills.
  • C) To explain why the library permanently cancelled evening sessions.
  • D) To identify the exact month in which all residents joined.
  • E) To describe a further evaluation before a decision.

Soru 17

A: Can we say that the programme caused the increase?

B: ___

A: I see. We need to examine other possible explanations first.

  • A) The increase never happened, as we both know.
  • B) The programme has already been banned in all regions.
  • C) There is no reason to collect information about the programme.
  • D) Not yet; several other factors changed at the same time.
  • E) Yes; no other factors need to be considered.

Soru 18

A: What do you mean by a representative sample?

B: ___

A: So its composition should reflect the population we want to study.

  • A) Any group that researchers happen to find convenient.
  • B) A group chosen after the conclusions have been published.
  • C) A group whose composition reflects the relevant population.
  • D) A group selected only because every member shares the same view.
  • E) A group that must always be smaller than five people.

Soru 19

Closest meaning:

Unless the equipment is maintained, its measurements may become unreliable.

  • A) Maintaining the equipment certainly makes its measurements unreliable.
  • B) If the equipment is not maintained, the reliability of its measurements may decline.
  • C) Maintenance guarantees that every measurement is perfect.
  • D) The equipment can never produce unreliable measurements.
  • E) If the measurements are unreliable, maintenance must already have occurred.

Soru 20

Closest meaning:

The policy reduced delays rather than costs.

  • A) The policy brought down delays, but not costs.
  • B) The policy reduced both delays and costs.
  • C) The policy reduced costs instead of delays.
  • D) Neither delays nor costs were affected by the policy.
  • E) The policy made all costs disappear before any delay occurred.

Soru 21

Paragraph completion:

The researchers first checked the age of the records. ___. This comparison showed that some supposedly early records were later copies.

  • A) They stopped examining the records before any comparison could begin.
  • B) Every document was destroyed before its wording could be read.
  • C) The team considered comparisons completely irrelevant to dating.
  • D) The records were never read or measured in any way.
  • E) They then compared the wording with securely dated documents.

Soru 22

Paragraph completion:

The system was designed to reduce staff workload. ___. This unexpected burden led the clinic to revise the procedure.

  • A) The clinic had no staff and never reviewed recommendations.
  • B) The system worked without requiring any human attention.
  • C) Every recommendation was certain to be accurate in all cases.
  • D) However, checking its recommendations took more time than anticipated.
  • E) As intended, it eliminated all staff duties immediately.

Soru 23

Irrelevant sentence:

(I) Farmers wanted to reduce water use. (II) They installed equipment that measured soil moisture. (III) These measurements helped them irrigate only when needed. (IV) Traditional farms in the region often grow several different crops. (V) This targeted irrigation reduced waste.

  • A) IV
  • B) V
  • C) I
  • D) II
  • E) III

Soru 24

Irrelevant sentence:

(I) The trial required all samples to be stored at the same temperature. (II) Staff therefore used a single controlled room. (III) This arrangement reduced variation in storage conditions. (IV) The team could then compare samples more fairly. (V) The building exterior was painted blue last spring.

  • A) V
  • B) I
  • C) II
  • D) III
  • E) IV
