Karma Set 01 — 20 Soru

Uygulama merkezi · Tekrar planı

İlk kullanım süresiz ve gerekçeli; sonraki yeni karma çalışma için başlangıç hedefi 35–40 dakika. Aynı setin süreli tekrarında cevabı hatırlama etkisini kaydet.

Cevaplarını, güven düzeyini ve kanıtını ayrı kâğıda yaz. Yanıt anahtarını bitirince aç.

Soru 1

The report aims to ___ the impact of reduced transport access on rural schools.

  • A) transmit
  • B) assess
  • C) inherit
  • D) emit
  • E) comply

Soru 2

  • A) preserve
  • B) deplete

  • D) abandon
  • E) contaminate

  • A) will be completed
  • B) is being completed
  • C) has completing
  • D) was completing

Soru 4

The institute is interested in ___ the study in a different region.

  • A) to replicate
  • B) replicated
  • C) to replicating
  • D) replicating
  • E) replicate

  • A) Whereas
  • B) Unless
  • C) Because of
  • D) Although
  • E) Even though

Soru 6

  • A) does
  • B) did

  • E) has

Cloze: The pilot scheme reduced delays. ___, it required more staff, which raised the total cost.

  • A) Nevertheless
  • B) Therefore
  • C) Similarly
  • D) For instance

Cloze: This additional cost must be taken ___ account before expansion.

  • A) on

  • E) into

  • E) they will be damaged tomorrow

Although the sample was large, ___ .

  • A) there were many people in it
  • B) it could never contain more than one person
  • C) its composition did not reflect the wider population
  • D) it therefore represented every group perfectly
  • E) its size was greater than before

Soru 11

Translate: Yeni bulgular önceki açıklamayı kesin olarak çürütmez.

  • A) No new findings can ever question the earlier explanation.
  • B) The new findings do not conclusively disprove the earlier explanation.
  • C) The new findings conclusively disprove the earlier explanation.
  • D) The earlier explanation disproved all new findings.
  • E) The new findings must have been disproved by the earlier explanation.

Soru 12

Translate: The changes may have resulted from irregular maintenance.

  • A) Değişiklikler düzensiz bakımdan kaynaklanmış olabilir.
  • B) Değişiklikler düzensiz bakıma kesinlikle yol açtı.
  • C) Düzenli bakım değişiklikleri önlemek zorundaydı.
  • D) Değişiklikler düzensiz bakımı gelecekte kesinlikle azaltacaktır.
  • E) Bakım yapılmadığı için hiçbir değişiklik olmadı.

Soru 13

Reading — Volunteer archive

An archive allowed volunteers to label digital photographs. Each image was reviewed by two volunteers, and disagreements were sent to an experienced editor. This arrangement reduced obvious labelling errors, but it did not remove all uncertainty. Some photographs lacked dates or location details, so even agreement between volunteers could not guarantee a correct label. The archive therefore kept uncertain labels marked as provisional. It also invited users to submit further evidence rather than silently replacing one guess with another.

Why could agreement between volunteers be misleading?

  • A) All photographs had complete labels from the start.
  • B) The editor never reviewed disagreements.
  • C) Two volunteers always have opposite views.
  • D) Users were forbidden to submit any evidence.
  • E) Relevant dates or location details were sometimes missing.

Soru 14

Reading: "provisional" most nearly means ___ .

  • A) permanently verified
  • B) unrelated to evidence
  • C) automatically deleted
  • D) subject to revision
  • E) certainly incorrect

Soru 15

Reading: The archive’s response to uncertainty was to ___ .

  • A) remove the editor from the process
  • B) replace every uncertain label with an unmarked guess
  • C) retain visible uncertainty and invite more evidence
  • D) hide all uncertainty from users
  • E) treat every agreement as definitive proof

Soru 16

A: Should we replace this uncertain label with my guess?

B: ___

A: Agreed. We should keep the uncertainty visible until we have more evidence.

  • A) Should we stop collecting evidence permanently?
  • B) Would it be better to mark it as provisional for now?
  • C) Can we claim that every guess is certainly correct?
  • D) Why not hide the uncertainty from everyone?
  • E) Did we already prove that your guess is impossible?

Soru 17

Closest meaning: The review questioned the claim without rejecting the whole programme.

  • A) The review challenged one claim but did not dismiss the programme entirely.
  • B) The review rejected every aspect of the programme.
  • C) The review accepted the claim without examining it.
  • D) The programme rejected the reviewers before the review.
  • E) The reviewers proved that no claim could ever be questioned.

Soru 18

Closest meaning: The data were insufficient to establish a causal link.

  • A) The data proved there was no possible relationship of any kind.
  • B) Causation was definitely established with no data.
  • C) The data were sufficient to establish every causal link.
  • D) The causal link prevented any data from being collected.
  • E) The available data did not provide enough support to demonstrate causation.

Soru 19

Paragraph completion: The town began measuring pollution at several locations. ___. These differences helped officials identify where further investigation was needed.

  • A) Officials stopped measuring before any readings were obtained.
  • B) The devices were never installed at any location.
  • C) Pollution cannot vary across different places.
  • D) The readings varied substantially between the sites.
  • E) Every location produced exactly the same reading.
