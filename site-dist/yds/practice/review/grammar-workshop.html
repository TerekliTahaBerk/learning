Gramer Atölyesi — 15 Kart, 45 Görev

Uygulama merkezi · Yanıtlar

Her kartta K kavram, Ü kontrollü üretim, S çoktan seçmeli vardır. Kart başlığını ilk öğrenmede gör; karma tekrarda kapat. Her görev 1; üretimde anlam ve ana yapı doğru olmalı. İngilizce üretimde birden fazla doğal yanıt mümkün olabilir.

Kart 1 — Simple / continuous

Referans

1K — Kavram: The team studies rivers ile The team is studying this river arasındaki farkı söyle.

1Ü — Üretim: We analysed samples when the alarm rang cümlesini alarm çaldığında sürmekte olan arka planı açıkça gösterecek biçimde yaz.

1S — Seçim: At the moment, the technicians ___ the faulty sensor.

  • A) replace
  • B) have been replaced
  • C) had replaced
  • D) will have replaced
  • E) are replacing

Kart 2 — Present perfect / continuous

Referans

2K — Kavram: Present Perfect ile kapanmış geçmiş zaman zarflarının ilişkisini açıkla.

2Ü — Üretim: The review began on Monday and is still continuing anlamını since Monday ile süreç odaklı tek cümleye dönüştür.

2S — Seçim: The analyst ___ four reports so far, and all four are ready.

  • A) had completing
  • B) completes yesterday
  • C) will be completing
  • D) has completed
  • E) has been completed

Kart 3 — Past perfect / continuous

Referans

3K — Kavram: Past Perfect neden bir geçmiş referansına ihtiyaç duyar?

3Ü — Üretim: Ekip keşif yaptığında iki saattir kazıyordu: dig / two hours / when they found the chamber ile kur.

3S — Seçim: By the time the inspection began, the workers ___ all the safety checks.

  • A) are finishing
  • B) finish
  • C) had finished
  • D) will finish
  • E) have finishing

Kart 4 — Future structures

Referans

4K — Kavram: Future Continuous, Future Perfect ve Future Perfect Continuous odağını ayır.

4Ü — Üretim: By next May / she / work here / ten years ile Future Perfect Continuous kur.

4S — Seçim: At ten tomorrow, the committee ___ the applications; the session will run from nine until noon.

  • A) had been reviewing
  • B) will be reviewing
  • C) had reviewed
  • D) has been reviewed
  • E) was reviewing

Kart 5 — Modals

Referans

5K — Kavram: Needn’t have printed ile didn’t need to print arasındaki farkı açıkla.

5Ü — Üretim: Elimizde sadece olasılık var: The error / occur during transfer cümlesini geçmiş olasılıkla kur.

5S — Seçim: The laboratory was locked all night, and no one had a key. A visitor ___ entered it then.

  • A) cannot have
  • B) must have
  • C) should have
  • D) need not have
  • E) would have

Kart 6 — Passive

Referans

6K — Kavram: Has been tested ile is being tested farkı nedir?

6Ü — Üretim: People believe that the building was damaged earlier → The building is believed… ile tamamla.

6S — Seçim: The instruments must ___ before use.

  • A) calibrate
  • B) have calibrating
  • C) being calibrated
  • D) to calibrate
  • E) be calibrated

Kart 7 — Relative clauses

Referans

7K — Kavram: Defining’de hangi relative zamiri düşebilir; non-defining’de ne olur?

7Ü — Üretim: The theory / the model is based on it → edat önde relative kur.

7S — Seçim: The expert to ___ the committee sent its questions replied promptly.

  • A) whose
  • B) that
  • C) where
  • D) whom
  • E) who

Kart 8 — Noun clauses

Referans

8K — Kavram: Noun clause ile relative clause farkı nedir?

8Ü — Üretim: Where did they collect the samples? → We do not know… ile dolaylı soru yap.

8S — Seçim: The decision depends on ___ the data are reliable.

  • A) what
  • B) which
  • C) whether
  • D) if
  • E) that

Kart 9 — Conditionals / wish

Referans

9K — Kavram: Second conditional’daki past biçim mutlaka geçmiş zamanı mı anlatır?

9Ü — Üretim: I did not save the file, and I regret it → I wish… ile yeniden yaz.

9S — Seçim: If the team had checked the valve, it ___ the leak; unfortunately, no check was made.

  • A) had preventing
  • B) might have prevented
  • C) will prevent
  • D) prevents
  • E) is preventing

Kart 10 — Gerund / infinitive

Referans

10K — Kavram: Stop doing ile stop to do arasındaki farkı açıkla.

10Ü — Üretim: The change will contribute to / reduce delays: doğru fiil biçimini yaz.

10S — Seçim: The new procedure allows staff ___ samples more quickly.

  • A) to process
  • B) processing
  • C) process
  • D) processed
  • E) to processing

Kart 11 — Participles / reduction

Referans

11K — Kavram: Having checked ile having been checked arasındaki çatı farkını açıkla.

11Ü — Üretim: Yanlış gizli özneyi düzelt: Reading the report, the conclusion surprised the editor.

11S — Seçim: Samples ___ at low temperatures can remain stable longer.

  • A) storing
  • B) are stored
  • C) have stored
  • D) will store
  • E) stored

Kart 12 — Connectors

Referans

12K — Kavram: Despite, although ve however takip eden yapı açısından nasıl ayrılır?

12Ü — Üretim: Although costs rose, the programme continued → Despite ile anlamı koru.

12S — Seçim: ___ the high cost of the equipment, the team decided to purchase it.

  • A) Even though
  • B) Because
  • C) Whereas
  • D) Despite
  • E) Although

Kart 13 — Prepositions

Referans

13K — Kavram: Result in ile result from farkını örnekle açıklayın.

13Ü — Üretim: price / increase / 10% / final level 110: by ve to ile iki ifade yaz.

13S — Seçim: All suppliers must comply ___ the new safety rules.

  • A) from
  • B) in
  • C) with
  • D) to
  • E) of

Kart 14 — Articles / quantities / agreement

Referans

14K — Kavram: Few ile a few; little ile a little tutum farkı nedir?

14Ü — Üretim: A number of students (is/are) absent. The number of students (is/are) rising: seç ve nedenini söyle.

14S — Seçim: The quality of the samples ___ essential to the reliability of the result.

  • A) be
  • B) is
  • C) are
  • D) have
  • E) were

Kart 15 — Word forms / emphasis / causatives

Referans

15K — Kavram: Efficient ile effective; sıfat ile zarf farkını söyle.

15Ü — Üretim: We had the technician calibrate the instruments → yapanı belirtmeden have something done yapısına dönüştür.

15S — Seçim: The team reached a conclusion only after the review. Choose the equivalent emphatic version.

  • A) Only after the review did the team reach a conclusion.
  • B) Only after the review the team did reached a conclusion.
  • C) Only after the review reached the team did a conclusion.
  • D) Only the team after the review did reached a conclusion.
  • E) Only after the review had the team reach a conclusion.

Karma kontrol

Kartları 2 → 6 → 12 → 8 → 14 → 5 → 11 → 3 → 15 → 7 → 1 → 10 → 4 → 13 → 9 sırasıyla, başlıkları kapalı olarak çöz. Yeni bağlamda transfer için Karma Set 01 kullan. Aynı kartın tekrar puanını ilk puandan ayrı kaydet.

Ek karşılaştırma — Reported speech ve nominalization

The researcher said, "I am testing the samples." ifadesini geçmiş rapora çevir, ardından The population declined rapidly cümlesini isimleştir. Bunlar puanlanan 45 görevin dışında isteğe bağlı transfer çalışmasıdır.
