Collocations — Sözcükleri Birlikte Öğren

Başlangıç · Temeller · Aktif hatırlama

İsmi bilmek uygun fiili garanti etmez. Kalıbı parçalamadan öğren; sonra özne/zamanı değiştirerek üret. conduct research ve carry out research yakın; yalnız biri seçeneklerdeyse bağlamı kontrol et, ikisini aynı tek-cevaplı soruda eşanlamlı çeldirici yapma.

Kalıp TR Özgün örnek Aile / kullanım notu
pose a threat tehdit oluşturmak Pollution poses a threat to freshwater species. threat to; pose a risk
raise awareness farkındalık artırmak The campaign raised awareness of energy waste. awareness of/about; raise nesne ister
draw attention to dikkat çekmek The study draws attention to gaps in the data. draw attention to; pay attention to başka özne rolü
reach a conclusion sonuca varmak The committee reached a cautious conclusion. reach/come to a conclusion; jump to conclusions aceleci
conduct research araştırma yürütmek The institute conducts research on memory. research on/into; research sayılamaz
provide evidence kanıt sağlamak The trial provides evidence of a short-term benefit. evidence of/for; prove daha güçlü olabilir
play a role rol oynamak Diet may play a role in recovery. role in + noun/V-ing
take measures önlem almak The city took measures to reduce traffic. measures to do; steps to do
meet requirements gereklilikleri karşılamak The design meets the safety requirements. meet/satisfy requirements; meet talep karşılamak
gain insight into kavrayış kazanmak Researchers gained insight into how children learn. insight into; gain access to
have access to erişimi olmak Rural schools have limited access to laboratory equipment. access to; accessible to
make an assumption varsayımda bulunmak The model makes an assumption about demand. assumption about; assume fiil
significant increase önemli artış There was a significant increase in output. increase in değişken; increase of miktar
strong evidence güçlü kanıt The records provide strong evidence for the claim. strong/compelling evidence; evidence tekil uyum
adverse effect olumsuz etki The change had an adverse effect on small firms. effect on; adversely affect
address an issue meseleyi ele almak The report addresses an issue that earlier studies ignored. address doğrudan nesne
allocate resources kaynak tahsis etmek Managers allocated resources to staff training. allocate…to; allocation of
reach a consensus uzlaşıya varmak The panel reached a consensus on the procedure. consensus on/about
undergo a change değişim geçirmek The region underwent a major change. undergo–underwent–undergone
impose restrictions kısıtlamalar getirmek The council imposed restrictions on water use. restrictions on
maintain stability istikrarı korumak The policy aims to maintain financial stability. stable/stabilise/stability
reduce the risk riski azaltmak Regular checks reduce the risk of failure. risk of + noun/V-ing
challenge a claim iddiayı sorgulamak New evidence challenges the claim that the site was empty. challenge doğrudan nesne
identify a pattern örüntü belirlemek The analyst identified a pattern in seasonal demand. pattern in; identify with farklı anlam

Geri çağırma

___ a threat, ___ requirements, gain insight ___, an increase ___ prices. Cevapları tabloya bakmadan yaz, sonra kendi cümlende üret. Sadece fiili hatırlamak yetmez; nesne ve edat ilişkisini koru.
