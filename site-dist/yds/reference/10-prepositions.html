contribute to katkıda bulunmak Training contributes to reducing errors.
lead to yol açmak Delays can lead to higher costs.
consist of oluşmak The sample consists of three layers.
comply with kurala uymak All facilities must comply with safety rules.
associated with ilişkili This pattern is associated with higher rainfall.

distinguish…from ayırt etmek The test distinguishes noise from useful signals.
susceptible to duyarlı / açık The material is susceptible to corrosion.
benefit from yararlanmak Smaller schools may benefit from shared equipment.
